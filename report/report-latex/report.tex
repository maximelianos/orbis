    \begin{abstract}
    We study the internal representation of the Orbis driving world model by
    attaching a lightweight attentive diffusion probe to its frozen image
    latent and using it to predict the future 2D ego trajectory. This report
    provides the background for that study: it describes the two-stage Orbis
    model (a hybrid VAE tokenizer and a flow-matching latent world model) and
    the proposed probing setup, reviews the theory of diffusion and flow
    matching and the diffusion-transformer architecture that underlies modern
    generative models, surveys world models for driving and navigation, and
    details the nuPlan dataset and the NAVSIM pseudo-simulation benchmark with
    its PDM/EPDMS scoring. The experimental results are reported separately.
    \end{abstract}
